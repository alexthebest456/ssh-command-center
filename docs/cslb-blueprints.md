# CSLB Examination Blueprints — Specialty Classifications

Extracted verbatim from the official CSLB License Examination Study Guides
(cslb.ca.gov/Contractors/Applicants/Examination_Study_Guides).

**Scope note:** These are SPECIALTY (C) classifications plus Asbestos (ASB) —
the sub-trades a **General Building (B)** contractor coordinates on a project,
NOT the B trade exam itself. The B path is its own trade exam (BStudyGuide.pdf)
plus the shared Law & Business exam. Reference for the Academy's Core/Finish sections.
